# Orca v0.1.9-alpha

Orca is a minimal local music player for Windows x64. This alpha improves browsing, playback and lyrics.

## Improvements

- Smoother library scrolling, bounded artwork caching and deferred cover loading during scrolling.
- Shared, exclusive and unprocessed WASAPI output, with clearer device-format errors and audio-path details.
- Improved 16/24/32-bit FLAC decoding and integer playback for supported devices.
- Refined full-player controls, queue dragging, lyric rendering, romanization and background choices.
- Organized settings, improved artist matching, composer/year browsing and advanced metadata editing.
- Quality labels show format, sample rate and bitrate. Whole-library artwork fetching is disabled pending further work; automatic and individual manual fetching remain available.

## Installing and updating

Download the Windows x64 installer or the standalone portable EXE below. Close Orca, including its tray/background mode, before updating. Existing library data and settings are reused.

Orca remains in alpha. Exclusive and unprocessed output depend on device support. Output details describe the stream opened by Orca; they do not certify the physical device or Bluetooth link. Linux is not part of this release.

Source code, including the modified FLAC decoder and software renderer, is available in this tag's source archives. Dependency notices accompany the installer.

Thank you for using Orca and reporting issues.
